Second architecture verified on hardware; no more self-introductions

Gemma 1B installed through the real pipeline - 1,011,738,880 bytes, byte
exact against the catalog - loaded as gemma3 with its own chat template, and
answered. On this 4 GB phone the memory plan shrank the context to 1024
automatically, which is precisely the situation it was written for. Asked who
it is: "I am Gemma 1B, a large language model created by the Gemma team at
Google DeepMind" - which is, unlike most small-model self-descriptions, true.

Two changes:

The identity prompt told the model what to say about itself, so a small model
said it - at the top of every single answer. The prompt now states the name
as a fact with an explicit no-greeting instruction, and answers came back
clean. Prompting small models is mostly about what you can stop them
repeating.

Downloads now show a progress ring in the chat top bar, fed by the WorkInfo
progress the worker was already publishing, and it disappears when the file
lands. Previously the only progress lived in a notification.

Also of note, verbatim from the same phone: Qwen 0.5B says a kilogram of ice
weighs one kilogram; Gemma 1B says 900 grams. Small models vary, and that is
a model property, not an app bug.
