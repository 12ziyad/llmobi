Site: stop centring the hero inside a two-thousand-pixel grid row

On desktop the split grid's row height comes from the model list, which is
thousands of pixels tall. The hero was justify-content: center inside that
row, so the headline floated halfway down the page with a void above it -
worse the wider the screen.

Now top-aligned and position: sticky instead: the headline starts at the top
where a headline belongs, and stays in view while the list scrolls beside
it. Narrow layouts were never affected and are unchanged.
